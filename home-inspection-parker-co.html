Why It Matters Here

What We Watch For in Parker Homes

  • Expansive soils. Douglas County sits on clay-heavy soil that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can move foundations, crack drywall, and rack door frames. We inspect for the early signs before they become structural repairs.
  • Radon. Colorado has one of the highest radon rates in the country, and it's the second leading cause of lung cancer in the US. A radon test is a simple add-on to any inspection.
  • Hail-worn roofs. The Front Range is hail country. We document roof condition in detail — often the single most expensive line item in a real estate negotiation.
  • Builder shortcuts in new construction. Parker is growing fast, and fast building schedules mean missed details. New homes need inspections too — often more than resales.
